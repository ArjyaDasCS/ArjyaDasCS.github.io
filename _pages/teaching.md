### ECS 140A: Programming Languages

**Teaching Assistant** · UC Davis · Spring 2026

Upper-division course on the design and semantics of programming languages. Topics included Go concurrency and
data races, term unification (union-find, occurs check), logic programming in Prolog, Common Lisp evaluation
semantics, and LL(1) grammars with FIRST/FOLLOW sets.

My role:

- **Assignment design.** Developed homework assignments, including a Prolog term parser in Go that builds a DAG with
  structural sharing, and a MiniLisp interpreter, along with their test suites.
- **Grading infrastructure.** Built and maintained batch, sandboxed autograding pipelines for Go, SWI-Prolog, and
  Common Lisp submissions.
- **Interactive grading.** Ran one-on-one interactive grading sessions with students across five homework assignments.
